# Hospital Operations & Clinical Economics Brief
**Focus Area:** Bed Utilization, Departmental Expense Concentration, and Transitional Care ROI  
**Author:** Rishi Dappu, Data Analyst

---

## 1. Inpatient Resource Concentration
- **Volume vs Expense Asymmetry:** Patients who were readmitted within 30 days comprised **26.8% of admissions (264 encounters)** but accounted for **43.8% of cumulative inpatient healthcare costs**.
- **Length of Stay (LOS) Cost Profiling:** Average length of stay for readmitted cohorts was **8.4 days** compared to **4.2 days** for non-readmitted patients.

---

## 2. Clinical Intervention Program Economics

```
Program Parameters (Modeled Simulation):
 - Transitional Care Outreach Cost: Rs. 5,000 / Enrolled Patient
 - Average Readmission Cost Avoided: Rs. 65,000 / Avoided Case
 - Intervention Efficacy: 25% of readmissions successfully prevented
```

### Scenario Comparison:

| Outreach Strategy | Enrolled Patients | Total Program Cost (Rs.) | Expected Readmissions Prevented | Inpatient Cost Savings (Rs.) | **Net Program Benefit (Rs.)** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Targeted: Top Quartile Risk (25%)** | 246 Patients | Rs. 12,30,000 | 23.5 Cases | Rs. 15,27,500 | **+Rs. 2,97,500 (+Rs. 2.98L)** |
| **Moderate: Top 50% Risk** | 492 Patients | Rs. 24,60,000 | 38.2 Cases | Rs. 24,83,000 | **+Rs. 23,000 (+Rs. 0.23L)** |
| **Blanket: 100% Inpatient Enrollment** | 984 Patients | Rs. 49,20,000 | 59.7 Cases | Rs. 38,80,500 | **-Rs. 10,39,500 (-Rs. 10.4L Loss)** |

> **Strategic Takeaway:** Blanket post-discharge outreach wastes Rs. 10.4L in clinical resources. Prioritizing enrollment by risk quartile turns an operational loss into **Rs. 2.98L net annual savings**.
