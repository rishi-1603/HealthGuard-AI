# Temporal Validation Prototype — Readmission Model
### What happens when you validate on the future instead of a random slice

> Generated by `scripts/temporal_validation.py`.
>
> ⚠️ **Date disclosure:** synthetic discharge dates, uniform over 2022-2024, INDEPENDENT of outcomes — method demonstration only.

---

## 1. The two protocols

| Protocol | ROC-AUC | PR-AUC | Train n | Test n | Test prevalence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Random stratified 80/20 (shipped) | **0.9923** | 0.9901 | 787 | 197 | 26.9% |
| Temporal: train past 70%, test future 30% | **0.9963** | 0.9939 | 688 | 296 | 25.0% |

**Difference (temporal − random): +0.0040 AUC**

## 2. Is the gap real? (sampling-noise band)

20 repeated random splits give AUC mean 0.9979 with a 95% band **[0.9934, 1.0]**. The temporal result falls inside this band.

> **Time-split AUC (0.9963) falls INSIDE the random-split sampling-noise band [0.9934, 1.0] — expected, because the synthetic dates carry no distribution drift. The protocol is the deliverable: on real data this comparison reveals exactly how much random splits flatter your model.**

## 3. Why this protocol matters (even with drift-free dates)

1. **Random splits flatter models.** Training sees patients from the same period as the test set. In deployment, training data is always older than predictions.
2. **The band is the honesty tool.** Without the repeated-split band, any AUC difference looks meaningful. With it, you can say 'within noise' vs 'real degradation' — the same discipline as the churn and forecast work.
3. **What a real run would add:** seasonal drift, protocol changes, case-mix shifts. Synthetic drift-free dates cannot show those — the deliverable here is the protocol and the analysis template.

## 4. Limitations

1. Dates are synthetic and independent of outcomes — no temporal signal exists to find. This is a method demonstration, not a temporal finding.
2. One model family (RandomForest) and one split ratio; the comparison is illustrative.
3. The label is near-deterministic given Condition (see model_honesty.md), so both protocols inherit that ceiling.
4. With real dates and real drift, expect the temporal AUC to be LOWER — that gap is the honest performance estimate.
